# Sample Leave Policy — Version 1.0
Effective: 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31
Status: Historical / Superseded

## Annual Leave
Fictional employees receive 15 annual leave days per calendar year.

## Sick Leave
Fictional employees receive 8 sick leave days per calendar year.

## Planned Leave Notice
Employees should submit planned annual leave at least 5 calendar days in advance.

## Approvals
A manager or authorized HR representative makes the final leave decision.
