# Sample Leave Policy — Version 2.0
Effective: 2026-01-01 onward
Status: Current sample policy

**Disclaimer:** Fictional policy created solely for the internship demonstration; not a statement of V7 AI Solutions LLP's actual policies.

## 1. Annual Leave
Employees receive 18 annual leave days per calendar year. Annual leave requires at least 5 calendar days' notice. Unused annual leave does not carry forward in this sample policy.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 sick leave days per calendar year. Advance notice is not required. Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible. No medical document is required for this synthetic demonstration.

## 3. Working Days and Holidays
The workweek is Monday through Friday. Saturdays, Sundays and designated public holidays are excluded from annual leave day counts. Optional holidays are excluded only when separately approved as optional leave. Partial-day requests are not supported in this first version.

## 4. Compensatory Off
An employee may earn one compensatory day for approved work on a designated non-working day. The record expires 90 calendar days after earning unless used earlier. Each comp-off record can be used once; expired records cannot be used.

## 5. Approval and Escalation
Leave requests require a manager or authorized HR user's decision. Managers should review requests within 3 business days. If a request remains unanswered beyond its approval due date, the employee may escalate it to HR. AI recommendations are advisory only.

<!-- page 3 -->
## 6. Team Coverage
A sample team coverage threshold of 60% is used. Requests below this threshold must be flagged for manager review, not automatically rejected. Critical deadlines and missing backup assignments must also be highlighted.

## 7. Privacy
Employees may view only their own individual records. Managers may view records only for teams they are authorized to manage. Attendance patterns must not be used alone to infer misconduct or make disciplinary decisions.
